# Extras

Footnotes[^first] point to a note at the bottom, and a second use[^second] gets the next number.
Text can be ==highlighted==, and shortcodes work: :rocket: :tada: :sparkles:.

> [!note] A note with its own title
> Obsidian-style kinds work too: `info`, `todo`, `success`, `question`, `danger`, `bug`, `example`.

> [!bug]
> Defaults to the kind's name as the title.

[^first]: The first note, shown where it is written.
[^second]: A longer note.

    It can hold a second paragraph, indented by four spaces.
